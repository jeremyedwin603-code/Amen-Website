FEATURES

A quiet corner for everyday faith

It starts with one scripture card a day: read, take notes, and pray — all in one place.

✦

Daily Scripture Cards

Open a new card each day and keep the verses you love, with 7 card styles that give each verse a face of its own.

❦

Bible Reading & Notes

Read the whole Bible, jump to any chapter by book-name search, bookmark, highlight, and write private notes — written for your eyes only.

❧

Prayer Pool & Letters

Place prayer requests in the prayer pool, organize them into groups, and send one prayer letter a day — one per day, to keep it intentional.

▤

Bookshelf & PDF

Import devotional materials or books as PDFs, add them to your shelf, and read offline with bookmarks.

◈

Reading Leaderboard

Compare accumulated reading time with friends and the world through Game Center — not a competition, a gentle reminder.

▣

Home Screen Widgets

Put today's verse on your Home Screen and Lock Screen. The card refreshes at midnight; one tap returns you to the app.
